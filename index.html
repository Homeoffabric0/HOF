ABOUT HOF

Luxury in Every Thread.

Good dressing begins with the cloth.

HOF — Home of Fabric is a Kano-based house for premium men's fabrics, native textiles and traditional materials, serving customers across Nigeria.

We carefully curate fabrics and distinguished accessories for men who value quality, elegance and timeless style. Our collections are selected with both traditional and contemporary dressing in mind.
